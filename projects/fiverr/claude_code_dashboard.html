The Process

How we build your dashboard

A collaborative, three-step process from first conversation to final handoff — fully documented and yours to keep.

1
🏠

Discover your home

We start with a short intake: which devices you have, how many rooms, who uses the system, and what matters most (clarity, aesthetics, automation depth).

2
🎨

Design your layout & theme

I sketch the dashboard structure, choose a color theme that fits your home, and propose a card layout. You review and we refine before any code is written.

3
⚡

Build & implement

I build the YAML, install any needed custom cards (layout-card, button-card, card-mod, etc.), and walk you through every section — clean, commented, and ready to extend.

4
🔧

Refine & hand off

After your first week of use, one revision round is included to tweak anything that feels off. You receive the full YAML and documentation to maintain it yourself.
