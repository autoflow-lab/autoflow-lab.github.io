What's inside every dashboard

Every component you see above is fully functional in the real dashboard — connected live to Home Assistant.

💡
Smart Light Control
Toggle, dim, and color-shift all your lights with a tap. Group controls, brightness bars, and 12-swatch color presets per room.
🎬
Scene Automation
One-tap scenes like Evening, Movie, Night. The Film Mode adjusts all Govee LEDs to match the mood — Warm, Cinema, Romantic, and more.
🎵
Music & Radio
Full media player with now-playing, progress bar, volume, and history. Built-in radio player with 6+ stations. Spotify playlist launch buttons.
🌤
Live Weather
Real-time weather via Open-Meteo — current conditions, hourly forecast, 7-day view, moon phase, and golden hour. Rain alerts with banner notification.
🔐
Secure OAuth Login
Full Home Assistant OAuth PKCE authentication. No tokens stored in code. Works locally and via Cloudflare tunnel from anywhere in the world.
📱
Mobile-First Design
Built for iPad and iPhone. iOS-native dark design, smooth animations, haptic feedback, auto dark/light mode. Installable as PWA.
